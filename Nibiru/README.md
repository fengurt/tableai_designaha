# Nibiru · 睿悦科技

Nibiru is an English-first technology IP for spatial computing, intelligent experiences, and product innovation.

## Brand Role

- **Main language**: English
- **Chinese name**: 睿悦科技
- **Tracks**: Spatial computing / Intelligent experiences / Technology products
- **Audiences**: Technology partners, product teams, spatial computing developers
- **Business**: Spatial computing, intelligent hardware, immersive experiences, product innovation, and technology services

## Voice

Precise, future-facing, and product-minded. Nibiru should sound like a technology team with real hardware, systems, and experience design discipline, not like generic futurism.

## Visual Direction

Use deep space blue, aurora mint, and violet signal. The visual language should feel intelligent, spatial, and high-contrast, with careful restraint around neon effects.

## Agent Notes

When generating Nibiru content, default to the English name `Nibiru`. Use `睿悦科技` as the Chinese alternate name. Prioritize concrete product scenarios, spatial interfaces, intelligent hardware, developer ecosystems, and immersive experience use cases.
